Our Services

General X-Rays

General X-rays are quick and painless imaging tests used to view the inside of the body, especially bones and certain organs. They help diagnose fractures, infections, chest conditions, joint problems, and other medical issues using a low dose of radiation.

Ultrasound

Ultrasound uses high-frequency sound waves to produce real-time images of organs and tissues. It is safe, painless, and radiation-free, making it ideal for pregnancy, abdominal, and soft-tissue examinations.

CT Scan

A CT scan combines multiple X-ray images to create detailed cross-sectional views of the body. It helps detect internal injuries, tumors, infections, and complex conditions with high accuracy.

Theatre

Theatre imaging provides real-time radiology support during surgical and medical procedures. It guides doctors with live imaging, improving accuracy, safety, and patient outcomes.
